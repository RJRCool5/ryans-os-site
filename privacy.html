Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026.

Ryan's OS is a personal app used only by its owner, Ryan Reventlow. It has no other users.

Google data

With the owner's permission the app reads his own Google Calendar events and Gmail messages (read-only) to show them on his dashboard. This data stays on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and it is not transferred to anyone else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
